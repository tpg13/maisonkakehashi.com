# Kakehashi Home Journeys

Create a luxury, high-end Japanese real estate consulting website for "Maison Kakehashi" (Japan Property Consulting - "Japan is closer than you think"). 

Design aesthetic:
- Premium architectural feel inspired by Belgrade Arbor: elegant typography, smooth scroll animations, refined dark pearlescent green (#0F241D / #163228) and warm gold (#C5A880 / #D4AF37) accents, washi paper and cedar wood textures, and refined Torii / bridge (Kakehashi) motifs.
- Hero section: Integrate the attached video (hailuo-03_A_hyper-realistic_photorealistic_render_sequence_of_a_residential_transformation-0.mp4) showing the house transformation, smoothly reacting to scroll down and up or ambient autoplay with scroll progress.
- Multilingual support in 4 languages: French (FR), English (EN), Italian (IT), German (DE), with an elegant language switcher in the navigation and full translations for the provided content.

Client specifications and structure:
1. Top Banner (all pages): "Possibility of covering part of your notary fees*" with "*depending on the case" small print.
2. Home Hero:
   - Headline: "Construction professional, Swiss resident, property owner in Japan"
   - Subtitle: "Maison Kakehashi — Japan Property Consulting. Japan is closer than you think."
   - Primary CTA: "Book a call"
   - Hero video transformation sequence.
3. "Your project, your way" section (6 interactive cards):
   - A holiday home for you: Your retreat in Japan, ready for every stay.
   - A holiday home rented out while you're away: Enjoy it during your stays, rent it out the rest of the year.
   - Short-term rental investment*: Tourist rental income, managed locally for you (*subject to Japanese regulations).
   - Long-term rental investment: Steady rental income, with local management.
   - Relocation or retirement project: Prepare your new life in Japan, step by step.
   - A base for long stays: Live Japan several months a year, like at home.
4. Services section:
   - Service 1: Purchase support
     * Step 1: Search & selection (understanding project, search, property analysis, shortlist)
     * Step 2: Purchase (negotiation, contracts, remote signing, help with accounts and tools needed in Japan, payment on the day of sale)
     * Tiered pricing based on property price:
       - €0 – 10,000: €1,250
       - €10,000 – 50,000: €1,750
       - Over €50,000: €2,500
       (Payment: 35% when contract is signed, 65% at purchase)
     * Renovation option: 10% of the works amount (minimum €500)
     * Introduction to rental manager: free
     * Actual expenses re-invoiced
   - Service 2: Listing check
     * Title: "Found a house online or on Instagram? Have it checked before you pay."
     * Included: translation, key points, pitfalls (land, rebuilding rights, year of construction, access, condition), risk zones, agency reliability
     * Fixed price: €45
     * Form: name, email, listing link, message + simulated payment checkout
5. My Story:
   - Story of James Loder: Japanese culture passion, crane operator background in France, Belgium, Monaco and Switzerland, renovation site experience. Bought his own house in Kōfu, Yamanashi Prefecture completely remotely from Switzerland. Acting as the bridge (かけはし - Kakehashi) between clients and Japan. Include curated architectural imagery and timeline.
6. Case Studies ("My projects & client projects"):
   - Real cases (Kōfu house in progress + houses analyzed for clients, location hidden). Format: title, photography, 3-4 lines, "Every case is different", with "Book a call" CTA.
7. Book a Call:
   - 30-min discovery call booking interface (lunchtime and evening slots preview / Calendly integration placeholder) + streamlined contact form (No WhatsApp).
8. FAQ:
   - Can a foreigner buy a house in Japan?
   - Do I need to travel to Japan to buy?
   - I don't speak Japanese or English. Is that a problem?
   - How much does a house really cost?
   - How does the support work?
9. Footer:
   - Legal notice, privacy policy, and prominent notice: "Maison Kakehashi acts as a consultant, not as a real estate agent". Brand details, languages switcher, and Torii bridge gold emblem.
10. Remove the "Tips" section completely. No discount percentage shown anywhere.
